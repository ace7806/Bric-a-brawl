Livery zone: Saddle blanket (the widest band on any fighter), front hooves. Body is always black basalt so every colour reads.

How it reads

A horizontal barrel with horns, on four floating hooves. The only quadruped and the only dark body: he reads as a black bar with a stripe of your colour.

Expression

  • Gilded brows, fixed. Emotion is carried by the HEAD angle and the nose ring.
  • Idle: one front hoof scrapes twice, the ring swings.
  • Charging: head drops 24 degrees, ring streams back.
  • Hit: head snaps up, ring lags behind.

Pose

  • Strikes are head-first; heavies rear up on the back hooves.
  • Stampede: body pitched 7 degrees down, hooves splayed in a gallop cycle, dust off the back pair.
  • Carrying a fighter: the victim sits across the horns, facing the wrong way.
