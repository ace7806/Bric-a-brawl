How it reads

A bowling pin with no feet. She never walks: she rocks, slides and rolls. The number of bands round her base is how many of her are left.

Expression

  • Painted smile, fixed. Emotion is carried by the WOBBLE: slow and wide when calm, fast and tight when angry.
  • Idle: rocks side to side like a weeble, 8 degrees, 1.1 s period.
  • Small form: rocks at twice the rate and never quite settles.
  • Guard broken: the shell splits at the seam with a pop.

Pose

  • Attacks are whole-body: bumps, bobs, spins. Mittens add reach.
  • Shed: the old shell tips over, the smaller doll springs 2.2 u straight up, mittens overhead.
  • Rolls on her side for Skittle: the face becomes a spinning blur.
